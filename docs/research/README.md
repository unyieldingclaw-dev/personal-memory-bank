# Research Staging

> **Staging only — not accepted PMB architecture or policy.**
>
> This area preserves externally discovered ideas, observations, and candidate lessons while PMB is being stabilized. Items here require deliberate review before being migrated into `memory-bank/`, standards, commands, or implementation.

## Workflow

```text
Interesting Shit → this PR → review / validate → accepted PMB knowledge → merge
```

- **Interesting Shit** is the broad capture basin for things worth preserving.
- **This PR** is the PMB-specific staging area for material that may affect PMB.
- **PMB memory-bank/** contains only conclusions that have been reviewed and intentionally adopted.
- Research may be rejected, revised, or moved elsewhere; presence here is not approval.

## Review questions

For each item:

1. What does the source actually demonstrate?
2. Is the observation relevant to PMB's current problem?
3. Is it an observed operational problem, a demonstrated capability, or a hypothetical future risk?
4. Does it justify a PMB change now, or only a future investigation?
5. Can the idea be adopted without adding unnecessary machinery or governance?
